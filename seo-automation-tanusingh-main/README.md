## Overview
AI SEO is an enterprise AI-powered search engine and content optimization platform. It automates technical SEO, generates Google and AI Search optimized content, audits AI citation readiness (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google SGE), formulates rich Schema.org JSON-LD structured data, and runs high-scale programmatic SEO workflows.

## Key Modules
1. **AI Search & Search Engine Visibility Scanner**: Evaluates URL readiness across Google, ChatGPT Search, Perplexity AI, and Gemini Grounding.
2. **Automated Meta & Schema.org JSON-LD Generator**: Instantly produces verified meta tags, OpenGraph social previews, and schema markup (Article, FAQ, Product, Organization).
3. **Content Gap & Keyword Matrix**: Discovers high-intent long-tail keywords, search volume, difficulty ratings, and competitor keyword clusters.
4. **Multi-Agent AI Content Production Pipeline**: 5 specialized AI agents collaborate to research, outline, write, and review publish-ready articles with FAQs.
5. **Technical SEO & Core Web Vitals Monitor**: Diagnoses LCP, INP, CLS, indexability, robots.txt, and canonical directives.
6. **Programmatic SEO Engine & Excel Exporter**: Batch-generates hundreds of location-based and intent-based URL permutations with instant `.xlsx` export.
